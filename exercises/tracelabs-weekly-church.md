## Note on source independence

The reverse image search returned four results across three domains — Wikipedia, a
travel blog, and a listings site (twice). All four display **the same photograph**.

This is one source propagated, not four sources agreeing. Repetition of a single
image across sites confirms that the image circulates with that label; it does not
independently confirm the label is correct.

Genuine confirmation has to come from something the photograph itself did not
supply — matching the building against satellite or street-level imagery on features
such as tower position relative to the entrance, the gate pillars, or the balcony
above the door.

---

## Limitations

- Identification rests on labelling attached to a widely-copied image.
- Independent verification against mapping imagery to be recorded.
- Camera position and bearing not established — only the subject building.
