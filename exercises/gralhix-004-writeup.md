# OSINT Exercise #004 — Island Resort

**Analyst:** Bohdan  
**Status:** Solved. Camera bearing outstanding.

---

## Task

Aerial photograph of a small forested island surrounded by reef. Identify the resort
and its location.

---

## Answer

**Oan Resort** — Oan Island, Chuuk State, Federated States of Micronesia

Coordinates: 7.36338, 151.75681

Camera bearing: [not yet established]

---

## Method

**1. Reverse image search.**
Returned the resort's own Facebook page, carrying the identical photograph.

*This is the strongest possible provenance for this type of question.* The image was
not merely labelled by a third party — it appears on the account of the business that
owns the subject.

**2. Name to location.**
With the resort named, searched for it directly. The about page on the resort's own
site gives its location.

**3. Coordinates.**
Confirmed the island on satellite imagery and read off coordinates.

---

## Notes

**Not all reverse image hits are equal.** A hit on an aggregator or travel blog tells
you an image circulates with a label. A hit on the subject's own official account is
first-party. Worth distinguishing these in any write-up rather than recording both as
"found via reverse image search".

**Two independent confirmations here:** the resort's Facebook carries the exact image,
and the resort's own website states the location. Both first-party.

---

## Outstanding: camera bearing

The photograph is aerial, likely drone. Establishing bearing would mean matching the
island's outline in the photo against its outline on satellite imagery and working out
the rotation between them — the reef shape and beach positions on the north and south
sides are distinctive enough to make this feasible.

Worth completing. Position plus bearing is a full geolocation; position alone is an
identification.

---

## Limitations

- Camera bearing and altitude not established.
- Date of capture not established; no metadata examined.
- Coordinates are for the island, not the camera position.
